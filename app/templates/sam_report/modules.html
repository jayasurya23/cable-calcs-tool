Drop the manufacturer’s PDF. We try to match it against the CEC database by name, then by its nameplate numbers{% if ai.enabled %}, and if neither places it, {{ ai.model }} reads the sheet{% endif %} — including a scan with no selectable text. Nothing is saved until you confirm what it found.

By hand

When there’s no datasheet to read — or the sheet is a scan and AI reading is off — type the nameplate values straight off the sheet. At least three of the four are needed: they’re what lets us recognise this module on a later upload.

From a pysam JSON

When you have the module’s pysam export, it carries the full CEC 6-parameter set — the most precise way to match it on later uploads.
